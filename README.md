This dofile was used for analysis of  Survey of Health, Ageing and Retirement in Europe (SHARE) data with waves of social network modules (SN). Data are available at: https://share-eric.eu/data/

Results were published in:
Menashe-Oren, Ashira, Damiano Uccheddu, & Ester Lucia Rizzi (2025)	"Social ties in old age: the effect of the COVID-19 pandemic", European Journal of Ageing, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10433-025-00889-3
